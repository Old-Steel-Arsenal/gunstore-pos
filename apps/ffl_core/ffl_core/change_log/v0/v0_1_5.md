### Version 0.1.5 — October 15, 2026

#### New

- **Lipsey's ordering.** You can order stock from Lipsey's alongside RSR and Sports South, and drop-ship orders to a customer or a transfer dealer turn on once the Lipsey's drop-ship login is entered in Lipseys Settings.
- **Check / Money Order payments.** You can take a check or money order at checkout, as an order deposit, when recording a payment, and when refunding an order; the check or money order number is required, and the money goes to the bank, not the cash drawer.

#### Improved

- **Lipsey's in the local catalog.** A store that runs its own product catalog instead of the hosted catalog service now gets Lipsey's products, stock and prices too.
